Data sources

Setup numbers and tuning rules come from these published references. They are cited, not copied as licensed content, and will be removed on request.

  • Base setups (dry & wet, all tracks) — GIGA, "F1 25: Trocken- und Regen-Setups für alle Strecken", Marco Tito Aronica, 1 Oct 2025.
  • Tuning directions — EA Help: F1 25 car setups guide · Traxion F1 25 setup guide (Ross McGregor, Jun 2025) · F1Laps controller settings · RacingGames.gg setup tips (Nathan Tague, 2022).

How the setups are built

One published dry and one published wet setup per track is the base. The article doesn't say if they're race or qualifying setups, so they are used as the qualifying baseline. Every other variant is derived with the rules below. The direction of each rule comes from a source; the size of each step is a heuristic that driver feedback calibrates over time.

    Known gaps: no published reverse-layout setups (the forward layout is used); GIGA's Qatar wet setup lists uneven rear pressures (RR 22.6 / RL 24.0, kept as published); game patches after Oct 2025 may have shifted the meta; F1 25's exact sprint parc fermé rule is not confirmed.
