    How these setups are builtRead this before trusting a number.

    Base: one published dry and one published wet setup per track from GIGA's "F1 25: Trocken- und Regen-Setups für alle Strecken" (Oct 2025), which says it draws on the author's own testing plus SimRacingSetup and F1Laps. The article does not say whether they are race or qualifying setups — here they are used as the qualifying baseline. Extracted twice and cross-checked; two mismatches were re-read from the source.

    Everything else is derived with the rules below. The direction of every rule comes from a source; the size of each step is my heuristic — your feedback is what calibrates them.

      Known gaps: no published reverse-layout setups; GIGA's Qatar wet lists uneven rear pressures (RR 22.6 / RL 24.0, kept as published, possibly a typo); patches after Oct 2025 may have shifted the meta; I couldn't confirm F1 25's exact sprint parc fermé rule.

      Sources: GIGA – F1 25 setups (Marco Tito Aronica, 1 Oct 2025) · EA Help – F1 25 car setups guide · Traxion – F1 25 setup guide (Ross McGregor, Jun 2025) · F1Laps – F1 25 controller settings · RacingGames.gg – setup tips from a sim pro (Nathan Tague, F1 22)
