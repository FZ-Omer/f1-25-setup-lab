    How it's built

    Read this before trusting a number.

    Base: one published dry and one published wet setup per track from GIGA's "F1 25: Trocken- und Regen-Setups für alle Strecken" (Oct 2025), which draws on the author's own testing plus SimRacingSetup and F1Laps. The article doesn't say if they're race or qualifying setups — here they're the qualifying baseline. Extracted twice and cross-checked.

    Everything else is derived with the rules below. The direction of every rule comes from a source; the size of each step is my heuristic — your feedback calibrates them.

      Known gaps: no published reverse-layout setups; GIGA's Qatar wet lists uneven rear pressures (RR 22.6 / RL 24.0, kept as published); patches after Oct 2025 may have shifted the meta; F1 25's exact sprint parc fermé rule not confirmed.

      Sources: GIGA – F1 25 setups (Marco Tito Aronica, 1 Oct 2025) · EA Help – F1 25 car setups guide · Traxion – F1 25 setup guide (Ross McGregor, Jun 2025) · F1Laps – F1 25 controller settings · RacingGames.gg – setup tips from a sim pro (Nathan Tague, F1 22)
